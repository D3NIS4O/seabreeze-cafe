Our Story

Sea Breeze Café opened in 2024 with one simple idea: a place where the rhythm of the sea sets the pace of your day. Our beans are roasted in small batches in Sofia, our pastries come out of the oven every morning, and our terrace catches the sunrise over the Black Sea.

Come for the coffee. Stay for the calm.
